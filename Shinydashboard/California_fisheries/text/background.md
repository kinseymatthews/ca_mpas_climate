## Welcome to the California Fishing Grounds Data Explorer!

<br>

### Why did we build this app?

This app was built to help researchers, managers, and the public 
explore how California fishing grounds have shifted over time.
<br>

### Navigating the interface
- **Exploratory** — explore vessel and trip counts by fishery and port complex  
- **Kernel density metrics** — view trends in fishing ground location, area, and MPA overlap  
- **Kernel density maps** — visualize spatial distribution of fishing effort  
- **Spatial predictions** — view model predictions under different climate scenarios
